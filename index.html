04Approach

A few things we refuse to negotiate.

A generic event is an unforced error. Below are the opinions that keep one from happening on our watch.

  1. i.

    Strategy before vendors.

    No one calls a fabricator until we agree, in writing, what the event is supposed to do. The brief is a one-pager. If it can't be one page, we don't have a brief yet.

  2. ii.

    Built with your brand team, not around it.

    If the room doesn't look like a glance at your homepage, we did something wrong. Brand, comms, and policy are in the kickoff — not the debrief.

  3. iii.

    Spend that compounds.

    Every event leaves a playbook behind. The fifth event of a program should be a third the cost and twice as good — or we missed something.

  4. iv.

    Measure what actually moved.

    Recall, sentiment, pipeline, hiring. Pick the metric that matters before the invitation goes out; report against it within ten days of load-out.
